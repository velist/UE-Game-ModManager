1. About this software

This software is a mod management tool for single-player Unreal Engine
games. Features include importing mods, enabling and disabling them,
merging configurations, checking conflicts, backup and restore, and
migrating data from older versions.

It manages lawful mod files obtained by the user locally. It does not
provide, distribute, or mirror third-party copyrighted content, modify
game executables, or bypass copyright protection mechanisms.

4. Privacy and data

- Core mod management works locally without signing in. Cloud sync of
  mod files is not provided.
- Application data uses %LOCALAPPDATA%\UEModManager\ and
  %APPDATA%\UEModManager\. You can choose a separate mod repository.
- If you opt into "Updates and anonymous statistics", the app sends a
  random device identifier, app version, and Windows version. A hash of
  your email address may be included when you sign in. A hashed email
  is a processed identifier; it is not necessarily unlinkable to an account.
- These statistics do not upload your plain-text email address, computer
  name, file paths, game folder, or mod list. You can disable them under
  Settings > General > Updates and anonymous statistics. No such reports
  are sent before you opt in.
- Account actions such as signing in, registration, and password recovery
  require an internet connection and send the account information needed
  for that action, independently of the statistics preference.
